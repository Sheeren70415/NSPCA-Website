Our Animal Welfare Projects

Discover how animal welfare awareness, cruelty prevention and community participation can help protect animals throughout South Africa.

Making a Difference for Animals

The National Council of SPCAs works to prevent cruelty and promote the welfare of animals. The following project areas demonstrate ways a website can inform the public and encourage people to support animal welfare.

🐕

Animal Welfare Awareness

Educate the public about responsible animal care, animal welfare and the importance of treating animals with compassion.

Visitors can learn about providing animals with appropriate food, clean water, shelter, veterinary care and protection from suffering.

🚨

Preventing Animal Cruelty

Help people understand animal cruelty and encourage responsible reporting when an animal may be suffering or neglected.

Members of the public should provide accurate information when reporting suspected cruelty. Reports should be directed to the appropriate NSPCA or local SPCA office.

Visit the Report Cruelty page
🤝

Community Involvement

Encourage members of the public to participate in animal welfare activities and support organisations working to protect animals.

People can explore opportunities to volunteer, support fundraising activities and share responsible animal welfare information.

Website Performance Indicators

The following key performance indicators (KPIs) can help evaluate how effectively the website informs visitors and connects them with animal welfare resources.

KPI What It Measures Why It Matters
Donation Link Clicks Visits to the donation page Helps assess public interest in supporting animal welfare.
Animal Cruelty Report Page Visits Visits to the reporting information page Indicates interest in finding information about reporting suspected cruelty.
Website Visitors Number of website visits Helps measure the website's reach.
Get Involved Link Clicks Visits to the involvement page Helps assess interest in participating in animal welfare activities.

Note: These are proposed website performance indicators. Actual results require website analytics or appropriate tracking tools.
